Choose your crew and conditions, then race the course. Build speed with correctly timed strokes, steer against the wind, and earn points for staying in your lane.

Plan preview will appear here.

Keyboard: ← / A = steer toward Bow (rower’s left); → / D = steer toward Stroke (rower’s right); Space = stroke; P = pause; R = reset. On iPad, hold the steering buttons and tap STROKE. The hull points forward along the course.

Stroke timing: the boat surges forward when the blades finish the drive and release, not at the catch. Keep steering gentle and counter the wind.

Scoring: points rise while you stay inside the lane and fall if you cross a lane boundary. Clean strokes build speed; missed rhythm slows the boat.
